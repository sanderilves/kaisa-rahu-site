Who I train

Training that fits your stage of life

Every session starts from your goal, your experience and how you feel on the day. These are the areas I work in most.

  • Strength training

    Learn the key lifts properly and build strength step by step, at your own pace.

  • Prenatal and postnatal training

    Sessions we adapt to how you feel. If your doctor or midwife has given you advice, we work with it.

  • Personal training for young people

    Good technique, varied movement and the joy of training – a solid base for life.

  • Posture and mobility

    Exercises that help you move more freely day to day and pay attention to how you hold yourself.

  • Training for beginners

    No experience needed. We start where you are now and build a solid foundation.

  • Not sure what fits you?

    Tell me about your goal and we'll find the right direction together. Get in touch

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I reschedule or cancel a session?

Your confirmation email has links to reschedule or cancel yourself. Please do it at least 24 hours before the session starts.

Do I need to be a MyFitness member?

You'll need a MyFitness membership or a single-visit ticket to enter the gym. If you don't have one yet, email me and we'll sort out the details.

What should I bring?

Comfortable training clothes, clean indoor shoes, a water bottle and a small towel. All the training equipment is at the gym.

What languages do you train in?

Estonian and English.

Can someone under 18 train with you?

Yes. Sessions for under-18s are booked by a parent or guardian using their own name and email address.

None of the free times suit me. What now?

Email me at tere@kaisarahu.ee and we'll try to find a time together.
