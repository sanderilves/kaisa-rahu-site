Privacy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This page explains what personal data is collected when you book a training session, why, how long it is kept and what your rights are.

Who processes your data

The controller is Kaisa Rahu, personal trainer, Tallinn. Contact: kaisa.rahu@myfitness.ee.

The processor is Cal.com, the booking tool used to book sessions. Cal.com processes the data on my behalf and on my instructions. Read more in Cal.com's privacy policy (opens in a new tab).

Bookings are saved automatically to my Outlook calendar (Microsoft) so I can see my training schedule.

What data is collected

DataWhy
NameSo I know who I'm training.
Email addressTo send your confirmation, reminder and links to reschedule or cancel.
Phone number (optional)So I can reach you quickly if needed, for example if a time changes.
Training goal (short)So I can prepare for your first session.
Chosen time and gymTo organise the session.

Please don't write health information (e.g. injuries, pregnancy, illnesses) in the booking form. We can talk about it in person at the session if needed.

The data is processed to make your booking and run the session, i.e. to enter into and perform a contract (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).

How long data is kept

I keep booking data for up to 12 months after your last session and then delete it. If you'd like, I'll delete your data sooner.

Under-18s

Sessions for under-18s are booked by a parent or guardian using their own name and contact details.

Cookies and analytics

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party tracking. The booking page opens separately on Cal.com, where Cal.com's terms apply.

Your rights

  • find out what data I hold about you;
  • have incorrect data corrected;
  • have your data deleted;
  • restrict or object to processing;
  • receive your data in a machine-readable format.

To use these rights, email kaisa.rahu@myfitness.ee. If you believe your rights have been breached, you can contact the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (www.aki.ee (opens in a new tab)).
